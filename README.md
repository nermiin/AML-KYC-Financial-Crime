# AML, KYC & Financial Crime Systems

Business analysis artifacts for **customer due diligence**, **transaction monitoring**, and **suspicious activity reporting** in a regulated financial institution.

## Purpose

Demonstrates how a Business Analyst bridges Compliance, Operations, and Engineering to deliver auditable KYC onboarding and AML monitoring capabilities aligned with FATF recommendations and EU AMLD frameworks.
